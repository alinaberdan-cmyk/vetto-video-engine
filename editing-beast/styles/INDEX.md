# Editing styles: index

How to use: when raw footage arrives **without a named style**, read the input signals below and pick the matching style, then follow its `STYLE.md`. When two styles fit, ask once and say which you would pick. Each style is built only from its own references. The only shared asset is the SFX pack `sfx-library/celf-sfx-pack/` (Meme Chaos brings its own meme sounds in `meme-chaos/sounds/`).

## Quick decision guide
- **Presenter-led, calm, premium** → Clean White.
- **Words-led; every idea needs a picture; product or app ad from a creator** → Cinema Lexicon.
- **Presenter-led, hype, finance/business/tech or an app promo the presenter explains, 3D/AI-heavy** → Neon Dimension.
- **Several people, one raw take, a game/contest/challenge, pure fun** → Meme Chaos.
- **One presenter teaching a step-by-step process with UI to show, calm, ends in "comment WORD"** → Butter Steps.
- **One presenter, playful series episode / personal-brand tip, has extra clips of themself for a montage, ends "ok bye"** → Star Glitch. (Numbered steps with a lead-magnet CTA → Butter Steps instead.) (Words-led creator ad where every phrase needs a literal clip → Cinema Lexicon instead.)

## Shared conventions (all styles)
- **Stock = Pexels** (Alina, 24 Nov 2026): whenever stock photos or videos are needed, take them from Pexels (start from https://www.pexels.com/search/food%20delivery/; free licence, never watermarked). Words that name something showable (food, phone/app, delivery, friends, places, objects) get a supporting photo pop-up or embedded clip, in the style's own way of showing pictures. The client's own photos and attachments come first.
- **Objects she names** (Alina, 24 Nov 2026): every concrete object she names ("an apple, a chocolate bar and a car") gets a picture of exactly that object on its word. Styles WITHOUT stock pictures (Butter Steps, Star Glitch) use a PNG cut-out pop-up; Meme Chaos uses a funny meme of the object (its §0c); styles WITH stock photos / clips (Clean White, Cinema Lexicon, Neon Dimension) show it with their own stock picture or clip, no cut-outs.
- **Never a bare edit** (Alina, 24 Nov 2026): every edit carries its style's music bed, effects, transitions and sounds as in the approved example; a talking head with captions only is never a finished edit.
- **Adapt, don't copy** (Alina, 24 Nov 2026): what stays exactly the same per style is the editing style, fonts, colours, transition effects and text templates (from its template/example code); never add elements from other styles; for every new video, analyse it first (who, setting, actions, what is said, mood beat by beat) and choose effects, set-pieces, B-roll, sound effects and music for what happens in it. Never copy an example's effect positions, music window, sound positions or assets unless they belong to the new video.
- Effects on words (27, incl. 8 text hooks): [`../effects/INDEX.md`](../effects/INDEX.md), same templates as the Vetto Video Engine.
- Measuring tools (18 Nov 2026, skill `beast-measure` in `../.claude/skills/`): study a reference video before writing or
  updating a style (pace, punch-ins, flashes, grade, colours, loudness, beat sync, storyboard → `styles/<id>/analysis/study/`),
  the beat grid of a song (put cuts and punch-ins on the beat), and the stressed words of a take (which word gets the key-word
  effect). The `vetto-video-engine` skill is linked there too.
- Transcribe with word timestamps. Re-transcribe unclear or second-speaker windows, and ask if still unclear. Every person who introduces themselves gets their own beat.
- Use client-supplied photos and footage first; stock only as a fallback (never watermarked, never with the watermark removed).
- Final names: `<style>_<topic>_<people>_<YYYY-MM-DD>.mp4`, saved to `finals/` and `styles/<style>/examples/`.
- Master audio to −15 LUFS, true peak −1.5 dBTP.
- Render phone (HDR) footage with `hyperframes render --sdr`. HDR auto-promotion breaks overlay captions. Always review a contact sheet of the *rendered* MP4, not only snapshots.
